Frequently asked questions

How do I connect my Google Calendar?
The assistant will prompt you to connect your Google Calendar the first time you try to use a calendar-related feature. Just follow the link it sends you in Slack.

Why can't a new team member use the assistant right away?
New team members need to be approved by your workspace admin the first time they message the assistant. This is a one-time step per person — once approved, they use it normally with no further interruption.

How do I disconnect my Google Calendar?
Revoke access anytime from your Google Account permissions page.

How do I remove the app from my workspace?
Your workspace admin can uninstall Daily Planner Agent from Slack's App Directory settings at any time.

Something isn't working — what should I do?
Email us at the addresses above with a description of what happened and, if possible, a screenshot. We'll get back to you as soon as we can.
