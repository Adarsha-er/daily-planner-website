Daily Planner Agent

Plan your day, manage tasks, and keep your Google Calendar in sync — all from a normal Slack conversation.

Daily Planner Agent is a Slack assistant that helps you plan your day through natural conversation. Tell it what you need to get done, and it builds a schedule around your existing Google Calendar — creating, rescheduling, and checking availability for events automatically. Each morning it sends you a suggested plan, and each evening it checks in on what got done, tracking streaks on your recurring habits.

What it does

  • Understands your tasks and priorities through normal chat — no forms, no separate app to learn
  • Builds a daily plan around your real Google Calendar availability
  • Creates, reschedules, and manages calendar events on your behalf, with your confirmation
  • Sends a morning plan and an evening recap automatically
  • Tracks streaks on your recurring habits and routines

Getting started

Click "Add to Slack" above to install Daily Planner Agent in your workspace. Once installed, connect your Google Calendar when prompted, and start a conversation with the assistant — try saying "hi" or "help me plan my day."
