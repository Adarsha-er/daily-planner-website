Privacy Policy — Daily Planner Agent

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Daily Planner Agent ("the app," "we," "us") is a Slack application that helps you manage tasks and your Google Calendar through natural conversation and slash commands. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

  • Slack account information: your Slack user ID, workspace (team) ID, and display name, provided to us when your workspace administrator installs the app.
  • Google Calendar access: when you choose to connect your Google account, we receive an OAuth access token and refresh token that let us read, create, update, and delete events on your Google Calendar on your behalf. We also store your Google account email address and calendar time zone.
  • Task and planning data: information you provide directly, such as task titles, due dates, priorities, and planning preferences, entered via Slack messages, slash commands, or interactive forms.
  • Conversation content: the text of messages you send to the app, so it can generate relevant responses and maintain context across a conversation.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not access Slack messages outside of your direct conversation with the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • To read your calendar and pending tasks so the app can generate daily schedules and reminders.
  • To create, update, or delete calendar events and tasks at your request.
  • To send you scheduled messages you've opted into (such as a morning plan or an end-of-day task recap).
  • To generate natural-language responses, which involves sending relevant conversation and task context to Google's Gemini AI model for processing.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Daily Planner Agent's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We access only the Google data needed to provide the features described above: your calendar events, your free/busy availability, your calendar time zone setting, and your Google account email address for sign-in.
  • We use this data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of the app, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • We do not transfer this data to others except as needed to provide the app's features (for example, sending task and calendar context to Google's Gemini API to generate a response), to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale with notice to you.
  • Our team does not read your Google data unless you ask us to, it is necessary for security or abuse investigation, or the law requires it.

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your data is stored in a private, access-controlled database (Supabase/PostgreSQL). Google OAuth tokens are stored so the app can access your calendar without asking you to reconnect every time; you can revoke this access at any time (see below). Conversation logs and temporary session state are automatically deleted on a regular schedule (conversation logs after 30 days, error logs after 90 days, inactive session state after 7 days).

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your data. We share information only with the third-party services necessary to provide the app's functionality:

  • Google (Calendar API), to read and manage your calendar events.
  • Google Gemini API, to generate the natural-language planning responses you interact with.
  • Slack, to deliver and receive your messages.

Your Choices

  • You can disconnect your Google Calendar at any time by revoking the app's access in your Google Account permissions.
  • You can stop using the app at any time; your workspace administrator can also uninstall the app from your Slack workspace.
  • To request deletion of your stored task, planning, or account data, contact us using the information below.
